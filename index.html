My Journey

The Intersection of Commercial Reality & Product Thinking

How frontline B2B sales conversations sparked my passion for solving deeper product challenges.

I started my career in business development and sales, where I gained hands-on experience in customer communication, lead generation, sales strategy, relationship management, and closing business opportunities.

While working closely with customers and businesses across the US, EMEA, and India, I became increasingly interested in understanding the problems behind sales conversations. When deals stalled or customers hesitated, the root cause was rarely just pricing—it was unmet needs, unclear onboarding, or product friction.

I wanted to understand how products are researched, designed, prioritised, launched, and improved. I didn't want to just sell what existed; I wanted to help shape what gets built.

This interest led me to explore Product Management and complete an intensive AI-First Product Management program through Airtribe, grounding my commercial instincts in user interviews, PRD writing, RICE prioritization, and metric trees.

Today, I bring together commercial experience and product thinking. I bridge the gap between revenue targets and engineering roadmaps.

My strength lies in understanding three things:

1

What customers actually need.

Uncovering root pain points through deep discovery interviews, active listening, and analyzing customer complaints rather than accepting surface-level feature requests.

2

What businesses want to achieve.

Aligning product initiatives with commercial viability, pipeline velocity, CAC/LTV dynamics, churn reduction, and realistic go-to-market execution.

3

How products and solutions can address real problems.

Translating user friction into scoped PRDs, intuitive UX flows, testable prototypes, and measurable North Star metric frameworks.

Fireflies.ai — Commitment Cards

The Problem

Fireflies.ai extracts meeting commitments but sends them as an unstructured broadcast email with no owner, due date or status; only 28% reach a resolved state in 14 days (proposed target: 45% in 6 months).

User Research & Insights

Conducted 19 recorded interviews across 3 companies (sales reps, team leads, founders) and tested Fireflies hands-on on a real client call; found visibility was the top breakage (8 of 19).

Proposed Concept Solution

Designed a card per commitment with owner consent, "Waiting on" tag, blocked button and one daily digest; prioritized 14 ideas with RICE and documented what not to build (concept prototype only, no pilot or real users).

North Star Concept: 14-day resolution rate (target: 45% in 6 months), with guardrails

VitaFit — Retention Strategy

Problem Identification

Severe user drop-off observed between Day 14 and Day 21 on the retention curve, eroding long-term user habits and customer lifetime value.

Root Cause Analysis

Traced the drop-off to broken-streak fatigue (missing a workout caused psychological defeat) and lack of low-effort recovery mechanisms.

Proposed Solution

Authored a streak-recovery PRD introducing forgiving milestone loops, smart grace days, and micro-session catchups with a 5% beta rollout.
